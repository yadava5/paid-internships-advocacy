Student-Led Campaign

Unpaid is Unfair

Payment for Placements is a student-run campaign asking universities, state and federal policymakers, and social work's two national bodies — NASW and CSWE — to fund student placements and to seat students, with votes, on the committees that set those rules. It is scoped to social work degrees, where an unpaid field placement is a graduation requirement rather than a choice.

Email Your Representative

Make your voice heard in government

Email Template

Ready to send 
Dear [Representative's Name],

I am writing to urge you to support legislation that mandates fair compensation for all interns. Unpaid internships create barriers for students from underrepresented backgrounds and exacerbate socioeconomic disparities.

The National Association of Colleges and Employers found that 40.8% of internships held by the Class of 2023 were unpaid. The burden is not evenly shared: 46.2% of first-generation students' internships were unpaid against 40.1% for continuing-generation students, and 46.5% for Pell Grant recipients against 39.0% for non-recipients. Fair pay for interns is not just an economic issue—it's an equity issue.

I respectfully ask that you:
• Support legislation requiring compensation for internships at for-profit employers
• Advocate for internship compensation reform
• Ensure federal internships are paid

Thank you for your time and commitment to student welfare.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]
[Your City, State]
